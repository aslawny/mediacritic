À propos de Start Marketing

Don't know how to start marketing your small business? Join marketing expert Katlynn Pyatt each week for authentic, accessible and actionable marketing advice. If you're a small business owner who wants to grow and scale your business, and you know marketing is the way to go, but you simply don't know where to start, Start Marketing will change that. Each week, Katlynn and her guests break down a popular marketing strategy and teach you how to put it into action. These short episodes are meant to make marketing actionable and accessible. No more feeling overwhelmed by the marketing jargon and unicorn promises you find when you google "how to market a small business". Katlynn is certified in Marketing Management by the American Marketing Association and holds two certifications from Cornell University; one in business strategy and the other in marketing data and analytics.
