À propos de Silk Digital Marketing Mastery with Samantha Brown

Welcome to Silk Digital Marketing Mastery, the ultimate podcast for ambitious business owners looking to dominate their digital marketing! Each week, your host, Sam, founder of Silk Digital, will provide insider knowledge and expert guidance on social media, email marketing, digital advertising, and more! Sam has over 7years of experience working with clients from startups to established brands, and her approach is all about empowering you to take control of your marketing and achieve your goals on your own terms. Don't miss out on new episodes each week! Hit that 'Follow' button now. And if you have burning questions, feedback, or ideas for the show, don't hesitate to reach out to us on Instagram @silkdigital_. Let's make your marketing as smooth as silk. You can follow Sam on Instagram @silkdigital_. To work with Sam & her team, head to www.silk-digital.com.au
