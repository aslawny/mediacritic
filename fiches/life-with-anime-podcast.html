À propos de Life With Anime Podcast

Welcome to the "Life With Anime Podcast", where you come to be entertained, find some escape and relief, and where we talk about the IMPACT that anime has on all of our lives. Our Hosts Joe and Preston, along with frequent guests, chop it up about everything ANIME & MANGA related. From the latest news, whats hot in the anime community, reviews on different series and genres.
