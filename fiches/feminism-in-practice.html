À propos de Feminism In Practice

A podcast that explores topics surrounding feminism with diverse groups of people who self-identify as feminists. The interviews will focus on exploring the following questions: 1. How do feminists define feminism and what are some of the various feminist perspectives? 2. How and why do people become involved in feminism? 3. What does feminism look like in practice and how does it inform people’s lives?
