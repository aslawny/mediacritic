À propos de Armchair Italy

Before you book the ticket, step inside the story. Armchair Italy is a fun, immersive exploration of Italian art, history, and regional cuisine that goes deeper than standard guidebooks—without boring you with dry dates and textbook lectures. Hosted by Anthony Capozzoli, each episode brings Italy's rich art and culture to life from Michelangelo, DaVinci, Caravaggio, and Bernini to the medieval walls of Monteriggioni and the history behind regional cuisine like Pane Toscano. Whether uncovering why "there’s no such thing as Italian food" or mapping out smart, insider travel strategies, this show helps you discover Italy before you experience Italy. Be sure to visit me at armchairitaly.com for help planning your trip and to find the best Italy has to offer.
