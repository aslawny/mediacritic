À propos de The Climb: Leadership Development and Confident Leader Podcast

Ranked among the Global Top 1.5% of podcasts, The CLIMB Leadership Development & Confident Leader Podcast helps aspiring leaders, accidental managers, and executives build the confidence to lead with impact. Hosted by Rahul Karan Sharma, Leadership Advisor to Staffing CEO's, each episode features CEOs, founders, executive coaches, and bestselling authors sharing practical insights on leadership, executive presence, decision-making, AI, workplace culture, and high-performing teams. Your title may open the door. Your leadership determines whether people follow. Welcome to The CLIMB.
