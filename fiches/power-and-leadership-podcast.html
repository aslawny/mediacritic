À propos de Power and Leadership Podcast

Andy has spent nearly a decade in power based crime intervention interviewing and working with hundreds of offenders and victims to rebuild a healthier understanding of partnership and power. In his second career as a corporate manager, L&D, and change management practitioner, he has applied the principles of power to help positional and peer leaders use power well. The foundational principles of his work can be found in his TEDx talk here (https://youtu.be/-eT9Zcff3WU). In this podcast he applies the principles of power in specific, practical ways that will transform the way you lead.
