À propos de ASK (Attain Secret Knowledge)

ASK is the podcast where we hope to help you 'Attain Secret Knowledge' with the assistance of our fantastically knowledgeable teachers from John Hampden Grammar School. Each episode we'll have three of our brightest KS4 and KS5 as panellists, along with a brilliantly knowledgeable guest teacher, to explore a topic that they love and hopefully you will too. Please follow to stay up to date with all our podcast news.
