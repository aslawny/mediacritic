À propos de Previously On with Jillian Bonanne

Follow for breakdowns, recaps, and funny reactions to the top shows of the moment! Hi! I'm Jillian and I host Previously On, with help from my friends and my husband. Each pod we review and joke about our favorite new TV shows. I mostly cover teen dramas, romances, book adaptations... you know the vibe! Posting full video on Spotify and YouTube. Currently recapping: Every Year After Elle Sterling Point OBX Previous recaps: Off Campus Euphoria Love Story: JFK Jr Bridgerton XO, Kitty School Spirits Tell Me Lies Heated Rivalry TSITP Instagram: @previouslyon_teentv YouTube: @PreviouslyOnTeenTV
