À propos de Agree to Disagree Show

Welcome to the Agree to Disagree podcast/show that contains discussions focused on current events, politics, and pop-culture. Every week I am joined by guests from various walks of life who are eager to share their experiences. As I strive to better myself, my goal is to learn from their perspectives and hope that you too can come away with a new outlook on issues that affect us. Let's come together as we discover new ideas and healthy debates. Support the show by buying me a coffee @ buymeacoffee.com/agreetodisagree
