À propos de L'Orchestre de Jeux Vidéo - OJV

L'Orchestre de Jeux Vidéo is a video game wind orchestra founded in 2008 in Montreal, Canada. As a non-profit organization our mission is to make video game orchestral music accessible to everyone and to offer our members and our audience a prime space to express their passion for video games. We stream a few rehearsals every month live on our Twitch channel, come take a look into our world!
