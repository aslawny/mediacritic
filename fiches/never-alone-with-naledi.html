À propos de Never Alone with Naledi

Life is hard (and extra). But you don’t have to go through it alone. Join me as we talk about anything and everything. From movies, tv shows, books to heartfelt conversations, weird things life throws our way and lessons I’m/we’re all still learning. I’m not really a serious person so I hope that this can be a space for you where we can hang out, talk nonsense and navigate life’s adventures together. And maybe, just maybe, it reminds you that in this vast and complicated world, you’re never truly alone. See you there 🍿
