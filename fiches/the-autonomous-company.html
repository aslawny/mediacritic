À propos de The autonomous company

Companies of tomorrow will have more AI agents than humans running the show. Hosted by François, founder of Pancake, The Autonomous Company sits down with the top 1% of YC founders in San Francisco - the ones already living in the future, with agents doing most of their work. How they build, what they automate, and the playbooks behind it. From Pancake (getpancake.ai), the platform to stack expert agents that run your business with you. Welcome to the age of high leverage.
