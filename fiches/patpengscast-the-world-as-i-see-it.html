À propos de patpengscast®, the world as I see it®

The Podcast is about travels, things to do and things not to do. But not only, it’s also about understanding the place we’re talking about and how it works, who lives and goes there and who doesn’t and why. During the show, I can welcome guests who have their own perspective and experience of the place being discussed, and they could provide details that I may not have. This means that this is not a travel guide, so get ready to embark on a captivating journey as we dive into the world as I see it®.
