À propos de Anime Me Podcast

Konnichi wa! Welcome to Anime Me! A podcast where one friend, Jo-san, show another friend, Sal-kun, the world of anime!!! Pop in those ear buds and join us as we explore the crazy, intense, joyous, heart wrenching moments that anime has to offer! If there is an anime that you want us to add to our ever growing list, drop it in those comments for us!! And remember to be nice, be cool and spread the love of anime!
