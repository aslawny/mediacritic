À propos de The Raspberry Pi Podcast

Welcome to The Raspberry Pi Podcast celebrating all things Raspberry Pi! Come and listen to a diverse range of expert speakers who will share their insights and knowledge on the latest and greatest uses, applications, and innovations involving our incredibly versatile single board computer and beyond. This is a must-listen for enthusiasts, developers, educators, and anyone curious about the power of accessible computing. Listen to the conversations with the people building it, from a team who can't stop thinking about it.
