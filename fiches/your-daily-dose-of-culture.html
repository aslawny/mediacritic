À propos de Your Daily Dose of Culture

Your Daily Dose of Culture is a podcast hosted by Connecting World Cultures (CWC) which is an organization with the goal of spreading cultural awareness and diversity. This podcast will talk about and explore cultures all over the world through interviewing unique people and telling stories. All of us have unique cultures and stories that we want to tell, this podcast will help bring these stories to life!

Catégories : voyage · culture · societe

📻 MediaCritic, c'est quoi ?
