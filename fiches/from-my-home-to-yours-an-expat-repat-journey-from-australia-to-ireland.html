À propos de From My Home To Yours: An Expat/Repat Journey from Australia to Ireland

Moving across the world to another country can be hard, but it's easier when your significant other is a local. "From My Home To Yours" follows the journey of Bernadine (Irish) & Matthew (Australian) moving their lives from Australia to Ireland. They leave no stone unturned as they discuss the logistics and planning needed to make the journey successful, and the emotional upheaval of making a new life together halfway across the world. And now they're back after two years away from the podcast and living their lives in Ireland, with a bit more to share about what being an expat/repat is like in the long term and what to expect a couple of years into your journey.
