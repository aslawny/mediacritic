À propos de The Goddess Way of Losing Weight; Meditations, Mindfulness & The Law of Attraction for Weight Loss

Meditations, Mindfulness & The Law of Attraction for Wellness and Weight Loss. The Goddess Way of Losing Weight starts with 21 day foundational episodes of daily intentions, journaling exercises and guided mediations with calming music by Jacob Cole. This wellness and weight loss podcast is a way to tune in, calm your mind & practice mindfulness to change your body and mind. You'll find peace around your body, food and in your daily life. Each meditation helps reprogram you subconscious and conscious mind. Each episode your sit, write, dream, relax and invite positive change into your life.
