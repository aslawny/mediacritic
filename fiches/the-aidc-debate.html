À propos de The AIDC Debate

The AIDC Debate offers a timely commentary on the AI economy and the fleet of data centers that enable it, all through a sharp technical lens. Manoj Sukumaran brings hands-on experience designing data center hardware for hyperscale cloud service providers. Shen Wang contributes a deep background in smart power electronics, data center thermal management, and electrical infrastructure. Vlad Galabov adds a strategic semiconductor-industry perspective shaped by senior roles at Intel and Ciena.
