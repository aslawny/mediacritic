À propos de A Venture Roast: Making Venture Capital Great Again

Lovingly poking holes in startups, Vc, and Silicon Valley. Venture Capital and Silicon Valley is broken. There are amazing people coming up trying to upend that model, and we're out here putting pen to paper on their visions for the future in long-form 1:1 interviews. If your preferred listening channel is outside of Anchor, and you'd like the reference links covered in the summaries of the podcasts, please go to https://anchor.fm/ventureroast
