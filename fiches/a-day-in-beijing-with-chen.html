À propos de A Day In Beijing with Chen

This is more than a travel guide to China. We are real people who live here, your eyes on the ground, and we want you to see it from the inside. A Day In Beijing is a weekly podcast about the history, culture, and people in China. China has opened its doors with visa-free travel. But most "China experts" have never lived here or don't speak Chinese. This podcast is none of those things. We're here to break the cultural stereotypes of China. It's 2026, if you want to hear real voices from China, you can now. Instagram: @a_day_in_beijing Email: urchineseconnection@gmail.com
