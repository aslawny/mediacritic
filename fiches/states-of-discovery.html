À propos de States of Discovery

Embark on an adventure with States of Discovery, where education and curiosity about the world collide. Our veteran adventure-loving hosts, Sara and Marisa, will take listeners on a quest to discover the country’s hidden narratives - one story at a time. From minimalist van life to podcasting live from the Wisconsin State Fair, this show will illuminate the untold stories of the world around us. If you’re familiar with our sister podcast, Not Your Average Bucket List, then you’re going to love this!

Catégories : voyage · culture · societe
