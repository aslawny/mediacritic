À propos de Trainsatlantic

Welcome to Trainsatlantic. Gareth Dennis and Justin Roczniak talk trains. Simple, unmissable, twice a month. Full episodes at trainsatlantic.uk, with every second episode free on here. Trainsatlantic is an E2M production hosted by Gareth Dennis (https://bsky.app/profile/garethdennis.uk) and Justin Roczniak (https://bsky.app/profile/donoteat.bsky.social). Theme music is Delayed Baggage by Ryan Stasik (instagram.com/ryanstasik). Follow the show at https://bsky.app/profile/trainsatlantic.uk. Full episodes are available at trainsatlantic.uk
