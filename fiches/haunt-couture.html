À propos de Haunt Couture

If you're obsessed with pop culture, the peculiar and macabre, gothic fashion, that's our specialty! Here at Haunt Couture, we deep dive into the wardrobes behind the infamous and lesser-known (yet much appreciated) horror characters. Join us as we discover why horror's been on the rise again, not only in the world of cinema, but through your favorite fashion houses too! Or, if you simply want to listen to two fashion nerds gush over all of these things, we don't mind either ;)!
