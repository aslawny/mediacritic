À propos de Balthazar NFT Gaming │ (Blockchain Gaming, Crypto Gaming, Play To Earn, & GameFi Interviews)

Gaming is being changed forever! NFT Gaming, Play to Earn, Blockchain Gaming, & GameFi will eventually take over the entire industry of gaming. While the NFT gaming industry right now is young and immature, there is so much ahead. Our goal is to bring you valuable content from the top leaders of the industry as it grows over time. You can expect weekly interviews hosted by Balthazar's Gaming Content Executive, Luke Jech (creator of Luke Plays to Earn), and Nicholas Korsgård, Chief Gaming Officer at Balthazar and previous coach for top League of Legends Esports teams.
