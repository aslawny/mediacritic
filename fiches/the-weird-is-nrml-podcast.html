À propos de The Weird Is Nrml Podcast

There's a misconception that People-People are soft. The best ones aren't. They're hard af. Listen to interviews with some of the best and brightest People-People and Leaders around. Get tactical tips and insights into what it takes to help others succeed. The podcast is hosted by Michaud Garneau, founder of Weird Is Nrml, a modern leadership development company. To learn more about modern leadership visit weirdisnrml.com or follow him on LinkedIn : https://www.linkedin.com/in/michaudgarneau/
