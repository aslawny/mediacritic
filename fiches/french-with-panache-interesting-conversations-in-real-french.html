À propos de French With Panache - Interesting Conversations in Real French

French With Panache is a podcast of authentic conversations about culture, society, and everyday life in France. Hosted by Violaine and Nathan, journalists and French teachers. For intermediate and advanced learners who want to understand how French is really spoken. 🌐 https://frenchwithpanache.com Hébergé par Ausha.
