À propos de Lights, Camera, Box Office!

Lights, Camera, Box Office! Rentrak's new podcast goes beyond the numbers for lively, insightful and entertaining conversations with the people shaping the theatrical movie business. Hosted by Paul Dergarabedian, Head of Marketplace Trends at Rentrak, and contributor Mike Polydoros of PaperAirplane Media, the podcast explores moviegoing, exhibition, distribution, filmmaking, marketing and the forces driving the box office. Each episode offers an informative and personal look at the movies, the business and the people who bring the big-screen experience to life.
