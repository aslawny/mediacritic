À propos de Unlearning Feminism

Grab a snack or sip you coffee while you unlearn your feminism. Wait, what? You read right. Unlearn your feminism- and make room for a better one. Every episode will help us unpack the loaded-ness of feminism today and reformulate it so we're helping all of the oppressed, not just the one's fortunate to get their voice heard. We'll talk about layered oppression, non-gender conforming folk, class, race - the whole gamut. Oh, and get ready for some theory. Are you ready to stop saying you're inclusive and start acting like it? We are too.
