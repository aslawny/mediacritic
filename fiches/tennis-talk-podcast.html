À propos de Tennis Talk Podcast

Come join in the discussions as we preview and review the tournaments on the ATP World Tour and WTA Tour week by week. We go through the previous weeks winners, all the match ups in the first round of the upcoming tournaments, have a look at the seeded players, have a look at past winners of the events and give out a special prediction on who you should back to win the whole event! youtube ► https://www.youtube.com/thetennistalk/ business enquiries ► info@thetennistalk.com
