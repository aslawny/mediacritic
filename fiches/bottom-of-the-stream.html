À propos de Bottom of the Stream

Welcome to ”Bottom of the Stream,” the podcast where Adam and Nick dive into the depths of Netflix and Amazon Prime to uncover hidden gems, overlooked movies, and streaming treasures you might have missed! Join them twice a week as they explore the vast libraries of these platforms. On Mondays, catch up on the latest streaming news, updates, and lively chat about what’s happening in the world of online entertainment. Then, on Thursdays, tune in for a deep dive into a lesser-known movie, where they provide honest critiques, fun insights, and spirited discussions. Whether it’s a quirky indie flick, a forgotten classic, or the latest streaming scoop, ”Bottom of the Stream” is your go-to source for all things Netflix and Amazon Prime. We’re here to make sure you never run out of quality content to stream—because sometimes, the best finds are at the bottom!
