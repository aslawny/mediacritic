À propos de The Mindful Leadership Podcast with Shahana Banerjee

What makes a great leader? Think of the best leader you’ve ever had. What makes them so memorable to you? What did they do? Did they believe in you before you even believed in yourself? Did they take a chance on you when you weren’t ready yet? Want to know why they did it? We dig into the why through deeply personal stories of great leaders who: ✅ Are Powered by their Purpose, ✅ Anchored in their Authenticity and ✅ Connect who they are to how they lead in good times and bad, at crossroads and crises ✅ who Challenge status quo & existing paradigms ✅ and Do different things ... differently
