À propos de The French Instinct

Go from B1 to C2 : real French that helps you go beyond intermediate level. Deeper, unexpected, beautifully imperfect, and human French for intermediate to advanced learners who want to move beyond textbook French and develop a real understanding of spoken French in everyday life. Through real-life stories, authentic anecdotes, personal reflections and cultural insights, you’ll train your ear with natural French that is beyond intermediate level. Each episode is designed to give you meaningful exposure to French that challenges you, while providing stimulating context to keep you engaged and encourage repeated listening. This is how new vocabulary, expressions and patterns gradually become natural. Imparfait, stimulant et profondément humain : ne te contente pas d'apprendre le français, vis-le ! Pour saisir toutes les subtilités des conversations de tous les jours et pouvoir réagir de façon appropriée, tu as besoin de comprendre le français familier et idiomatique, l'argot, l'humour.…
