À propos de Dive Around The World

The podcast for all ocean lovers! Listen to inspiring stories about diving, freediving, swimming, lifeguarding, mermaiding and come explore the world's most amazing dive sites and dive travel destinations with us. We talk about our passion for the underwater world and about efforts to protect marine areas and the life in it. Monthly episodes sponsored by MyDiveGuide (powered by SSI), the world´s most amazing dive spot database connecting top dive sites and their potential wildlife encounters with the SSI Training Centers which can take you there.

Catégories : voyage · culture · societe
