À propos de Tennis Lounge (podcast en français)

🇬🇧Tennis Lounge is a tennis podcast focused on mental performance, how to build your tennis career, and the reasons why tennis influence extends far beyond the boundaries of sport. On Tennis Lounge, you'll find: -In-depth analyses of the mental strategies and performance habits of some of the greatest champions in the game, including Novak Djokovic, Serena Williams, Carlos Alcaraz, Jannik Sinner, Rafael Nadal, Coco Gauff, Roger Federer, Iga Świątek, and many more; -Interviews with international coaches working across the junior, ITF, ATP, and WTA circuits, as well as experts sharing practical insights on how to better navigate and manage a tennis career and the mistakes to avoid; -The stories that have shaped the history of the sport, and an exploration of why tennis remains a powerful force of influence beyond the court. The podcast is hosted by Jennifer Migan, a former professional tennis player who competed on the ITF circuit (career-high ranking inside the world's Top 800) and in NCAA Division I tennis (Top 50 in singles and Top 25 in doubles). She's a university professor, a storyteller and a communication consultant.…
