À propos de Des nouvelles du 12 bis: the podcast in French-English

How are the neighbours at 12 bis doing today? Two years after arriving in Paris, Billie updates Rosa, who's travelling in her country. She tells her about Zirek’s reunion with his family. Diane, Pierre, Amir and a new neighbour are helping them settle into their new life. "Des Nouvelles du 12 bis", a bilingual radio story in immersive listening to help you learn French through everyday life situations.
