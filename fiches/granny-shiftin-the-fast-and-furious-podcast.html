À propos de Granny Shiftin’: THE Fast and Furious podcast

The Fast and The Furious podcast that takes the world’s fastest movie and slows it down to one furious minute at a time. We're taking the Fast and the Furious movies one at a time and looking at each minute, one by one, to find all the hidden secrets and amazing moments that make up this series. Watch each minute on instagram at https://www.instagram.com/grannyshiftpod/ And remember....
