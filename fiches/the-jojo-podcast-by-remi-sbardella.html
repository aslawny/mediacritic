À propos de The JOJO Podcast by Remi Sbardella

Hello and welcome ! My name is Remi, I am the founder and creator of @myjojofr. The JOJO Podcast is a collection of intimate and personal conversations, a space where each guest is safe to have "big talks". In each episode, we deep dive into parts of my guest's unique and complex combination of social and political identities to better understand their vision of themselves, the world and how they experience it all. We sometimes get emotional, we sometimes laugh, I promise that each episode is an emotional roller-coaster for the heart and the soul. Enjoy !
