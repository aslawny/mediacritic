À propos de Crypto Nuggets - Exploring the most innovative projects in blockchain

Exploring the most innovative crypto projects in the space. We talk with teams and investors, focusing on the problems these products actually solve, and how they can serve users. Too often in crypto we see projects with cool tech that don't actually have a purpose in the world or solve a real problem. On Crypto Nuggets, we focus on the customer, users, problem to solve, why it matters, what value it provides, and what success looks like. Topics include Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, Decentralized Finance (DeFi), NFTs, Liquid Staking, Decentralized Compute, Layer 1, Layer 2, DAOs
