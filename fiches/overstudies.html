À propos de Overstudies

Chaos-filled, London-based duo Bekki and Charley overanalyse theatre, musicals and everything in between. Overstudies is a space to appreciate theatre purely for the joy of appreciating theatre, where we celebrate the unsung understudies, covers and swings and discuss lesser-covered topics affecting the industry today.
