À propos de T's Storytime

I LOVE folklore, lore, and generally local weirdness. If it makes a place unique, I want in! All business: tsstorytimeinbox@gmail.com This channel began exploring urban legends and local mythology in the united states, and while that's still our primary focus here, we're also very interested in history, culture, and perhaps even the paranormal. Whether you've got an urban legend that needs explanation or a local UFO case that's gone uncovered you can be sure we'll cover it here. Want to support the channel? https://buymeacoffee.com/tsstorytime
