À propos de Tennis Volley Talk with Brian Danielson

Talking Tennis and Racquets with stories that I have had in Tennis. Look out for the "Tennis Racquet of the Day", and talk about tennis superstars including Jimmy Conners, Bjorn Borg, John McEnroe, Poncho Gonzales, Rod Laver, and more. Everything from Tennis shoes from Adidas, K-Swiss, and Nike, to the woman's movement and Billie Jean King, who paved the way into tennis for Everyone. Hosted by Brian Danielson.
