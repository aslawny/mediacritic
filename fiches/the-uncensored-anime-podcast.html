À propos de The Uncensored Anime Podcast

Welcome to the Uncensored Anime Podcast! Every week these 3 beautiful weebs get together to chat about 1-2 anime series, could be old, could be new, but what we know is that we are going to talk about it with NO CENSORING. The goal is for us to enjoy more anime that we otherwise may not have and for you all to follow along and let us know your thoughts! This is THE UNCENSORED ANIME PODCAST Main Hosts: Jerry THE ANIME ENCYCLOPEDIA Kenny THE "I'LL WATCH ANY ANIME" GUY Thibby THE NAIVE ANIME WATCHER
